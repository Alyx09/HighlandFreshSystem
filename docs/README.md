# Highland Fresh Dairy Cooperative System

## About the Project
The Highland Fresh Dairy Cooperative System is a web-based Sales, Production, and Inventory Management System developed as our BSIT capstone project. It aims to improve business operations by organizing records, monitoring inventory, managing production, and tracking product information efficiently.

## Features
- **Dashboard** – Provides an overview of sales, production, and inventory.
- **Raw Milk Intake and Quality Control** – Records incoming raw milk and its quality inspection results.
- **Production Management** – Monitors production batches, ingredients, outputs, and wastage.
- **Inventory Management** – Tracks raw materials and finished products.
- **Sales Management** – Records sales transactions and product dispatches.
- **Product Traceability** – Tracks products from raw material intake through production to finished goods.
- **Reports and Analytics** – Provides information to support monitoring and decision-making.
